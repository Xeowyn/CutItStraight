Odd finds

Each column is one find (pre-rendered by tools/gen_finds.py); each row is a different work-light direction — top: the find is far up the board and lit from the blade's side; middle: lamp off to one side; third: sitting directly under the lamp, lit flat with almost no cast shadow. The BOTTOM row is the same finds SAWN IN HALF, which is what happens to one the blade goes through. What to check: the highlight is on the lamp side in every row, the shadow points away from it, every object is nameable from its outline alone, and every sawn half is a half of the object rather than a clipped picture of it.
